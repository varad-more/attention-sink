# Demo script

Ninety seconds, spoken, against the live site. Every number said out loud is on the
screen while it is said.

---

**[0:00 — /cycle/24]**

"Six agents. They started with the same twelve memories, got the same twenty-four
events, ran on the same model, and had the same two hundred and eight tokens of room.
One thing differs: what each one throws away when that room runs out."

**[0:12 — scroll to Goldfish and Present-Minded]**

"Cycle twenty-four. Same event, same day. Present-Minded says 'My name is Mara Venn, and
I came to Station Kestrel to find Ivo.' Goldfish, next to it, is writing about a room and
a recorder and can't say who it is."

**[0:28 — /graveyard?sort=oldest]**

"Here's why. This is the Graveyard — every memory that left an active set. Sorted oldest
first, and the very first record is Goldfish's own name. Born cycle zero, retired cycle
four, zero citations, reason: evicted oldest. You can read it. The agent can't."

**[0:45 — /echoes?category=partial_reconstruction]**

"Sometimes something comes back. This new memory sits closer to a memory the mind has
lost — 0.393 — than to anything it still holds — 0.101. That's a measured distance, not
an access. The page says so, and so do I: the stimulus that cycle was about clocks, which
is a plainer explanation than reconstruction."

**[1:02 — /interviews?cycle=24&question=q01]**

"Same question to all six: who are you? Three still give the name. Goldfish says it's an
AI system built by a team of inventors at Amazon. At cycle zero, all six answered
identically."

**[1:18 — the results chart, or /timeline]**

"Recall went from one-point-zero for everyone to a spread of zero-point-five down to
zero. And the honest headline: the random control beat three of the five designed
mechanisms. One run, one seed. The mechanisms diverge. Which one is better, this doesn't
say."

**[1:30 — end]**

"It runs on EventBridge Scheduler, Lambda and Bedrock, commits six arms atomically to
DynamoDB, and the whole dataset is downloadable with checksums. Twenty cents."

## If you have thirty seconds instead

Open `/graveyard?sort=oldest`. Say: "The first thing any of these six agents forgot was
its own name — evicted at cycle four because it was the oldest thing it held and nothing
in that mechanism protects a name. Twenty cycles later it introduced itself as an AI
system built by Amazon. Its five siblings, same model, same day, three of them still knew
who they were. The only difference between them is what they throw away."

## Things not to say

- Not "the model remembered a deleted memory." It cannot and did not.
- Not "the best policy." One run, one seed world, and the random control outscored three designed arms.
- Not "attention sink" as a claim about the model's internals. The name is a metaphor and the project says so.
