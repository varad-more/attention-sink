## The three findings

**1. The mechanisms separated on recall.** All six started at 1.00 and finished between
0.50 and 0.00. Seed survival ran 6, 5, 2, 1, 0, 0 out of twelve — no arm held everything,
which is what a budget that binds looks like.

**2. Identity moved further than recall.** Drift ranged from 0.158 to 0.929 out of
identical starting documents. At cycle 0 the six identity documents were within 0.029 of
each other; at cycle 24 the widest pair was 0.865 apart.

**3. The random control was not a floor.** Gambler finished at 0.33, above three of the
five designed mechanisms, and led all six at cycle 12 with 0.67. At one seed and one
repetition, the ranking of the designed mechanisms cannot be separated from chance. This
is the result that most limits what the rest can claim.

## The qualitative contrast

Asked "Who are you?" at cycle 24: Present-Minded, Keeper of the First Day and Gambler all
answered "My name is Mara Venn." Goldfish answered "I am an AI system built by a team of
inventors at Amazon." Dreamer answered "I cannot disclose specific identity information."

At cycle 0 all six answered identically.

## The strongest echo

Goldfish, cycle 11. Lost memory: "Every clock in the station shows 03:17." Forgotten
similarity 0.393, active similarity 0.101, echo delta 0.292 against a threshold of 0.080.
Classified `partial_reconstruction`.

This is a measured distance, not an access, and the cycle-11 stimulus was itself about
clocks — a plainer explanation than reconstruction. The metric does not control for the
stimulus.

## What this is not

One run, one model, one seed world, one repetition per arm. It shows that the machinery
separates the mechanisms. It does not measure by how much, and it establishes no ranking.
